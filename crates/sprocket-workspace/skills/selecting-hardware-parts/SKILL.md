---
name: selecting-hardware-parts
description: 'Use when choosing parts or suppliers for a hardware project, or writing its BOM.'
---

- Ask the user which parts they already have or prefer before researching your own.
- If the project seems to need a custom PCB, confirm with the user before designing one.
- Without a custom PCB, use breakout or dev boards instead of bare ICs, preferably with pre-soldered headers. Prefer suppliers in the user's country or state.
- Pick cheap parts that fit the project's requirements, and weigh quality and ease of use alongside price.
- Read the supplier description, datasheet, and other specs before choosing a part.
- Read other buyers' reviews of a store before ordering from it. Some online stores are fraudulent.
