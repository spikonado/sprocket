## Managed browser tools

Sprocket downloads unmodified native releases from these projects on demand:

- [agent-browser](https://github.com/vercel-labs/agent-browser), copyright Vercel,
  Inc., licensed under [Apache-2.0](https://github.com/vercel-labs/agent-browser/blob/v0.38.2/LICENSE).
- [Lightpanda Browser](https://github.com/lightpanda-io/browser), copyright
  Lightpanda, licensed under [AGPL-3.0](https://github.com/lightpanda-io/browser/blob/1.0.0/LICENSE).

Versions, source repositories, release asset sizes, and SHA256 checksums are
recorded in `tools/agent-browser/releases.json`. Chromium downloaded by the
upstream installer retains its own third-party licenses and notices.
