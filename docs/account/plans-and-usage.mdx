title: Plans and usage

---

Each plan includes a dollar amount of AI usage per month for models through the Spikonado provider. There are no session, daily, or weekly caps. See current plans and prices on the [pricing page](https://usesprocket.dev/pricing).

## When usage runs out

- Buy a credit pack on the [pricing page](https://usesprocket.dev/pricing). Sprocket uses credit packs after your monthly usage runs out. The pricing page shows when each pack expires.
- Upgrade your plan.
- Connect your own [API keys or subscriptions](/use/models-and-providers#providers). They don't use your Sprocket AI usage.

## Track usage

| Where                          | What it shows                                                                                       |
| ------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Settings → Account**         | Your plan's usage, when it resets, and your credit pack balance                                     |
| **Settings → AI Usage**        | Cost, tokens, cache hit rate, and model calls over a time range, with a chart and a per-model table |
| A thread's **AI usage** button | The cost of that thread and its subagents                                                           |

Costs in **Settings → AI Usage** and the thread button use direct API prices for every provider. They include models you pay for with your own keys.

## Billing

Manage a paid plan with **Manage billing** on the [pricing page](https://usesprocket.dev/pricing).
