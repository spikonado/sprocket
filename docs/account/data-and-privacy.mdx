---
title: Data and privacy
description: How Sprocket keeps your code, conversations, and keys private and encrypted.
---

Your project files stay on your computer. The agent reads and edits them in place, and Sprocket's servers see a file only when the agent reads it into a thread. Spikonado doesn't sell your data. Read the full [privacy policy](https://spikonado.com/privacy).

## Encryption

- Everything between your computer and Sprocket's servers travels over TLS.
- Threads, artifacts, and account data are stored in [Convex](https://www.convex.dev/security), encrypted at rest with AES-256.
- Attachments are stored in [Cloudflare R2](https://developers.cloudflare.com/r2/reference/data-security/), encrypted at rest with AES-256.
- API keys and Claude sign-ins are encrypted in [WorkOS Vault](https://workos.com/docs/vault). Each one gets its own encryption key, which is protected by a key that never leaves a hardware security module.
- Your Sprocket, ChatGPT, GitHub, and Vercel sign-ins are stored only on your computer.

## Secrets in threads

Before a thread leaves your computer, Sprocket replaces API keys, passwords, and private keys in your messages and tool output with placeholders like `[redacted:openai-key]`.

## Usage analytics

Sprocket sends usage numbers, such as token counts and how long runs take, to [PostHog](https://posthog.com). It doesn't send your prompts, the agent's replies, or your files. Session replays mask all text.

## Deleting data

You can delete threads, artifacts, and saved keys at any time. Deleting an artifact also deletes its file in your project. Sprocket deletes threads after 180 days without activity. To delete your account, email [aarav@spikonado.com](mailto:aarav@spikonado.com).
