---
title: Purchases
description: Let Sprocket's agent buy parts and subscriptions at online checkouts with your saved card, approving purchases with your passkey.
---

The agent can pay at an online checkout with a card you saved in Sprocket. You approve each purchase with your passkey, unless you turned on auto-approval in Agentcard. [Agentcard](https://agentcard.sh) stores your cards, and Sprocket never sees the card number.

## Save a card

Open **Settings → Payments** and click **Add a card**. Scan the QR code with your phone and save the card on Agentcard's page with Face ID or your fingerprint.

Agentcard locks your card with a passkey. If your computer has one, like Touch ID, Windows Hello, or a password manager, you can click **Open on this computer** instead.

## How a purchase works

<Steps>
	<Step title="The agent shops">
		The agent opens the store in its browser and confirms the items, quantity, shipping address,
		shipping method, and total with you.
	</Step>
	<Step title="You approve">
		The thread shows the amount and the store, such as **Approve $24.99 at Adafruit**. Click
		**Approve**, scan the QR code with the phone or open it on the computer that holds your passkey,
		and confirm. Purchases that Agentcard auto-approves skip this step.
	</Step>
	<Step title="Agentcard pays">
		Agentcard fills in your real card and Sprocket submits the order. The agent then reports the
		order number, or the store's error if payment failed.
	</Step>
</Steps>

The checkout page must use HTTPS. If you stop the run while a purchase waits for approval, Sprocket cancels the purchase.

The agent can only start a purchase in a thread you started from the app. Subagents and `sprocket run` tasks can't start purchases.

## Purchase history

**Settings → Payments** lists your last 100 purchases with their status, such as Awaiting approval, Declined, Expired, Cancelled, or Completed.
