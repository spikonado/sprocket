---
title: Purchases
description: Save a card, approve online purchases with your passkey, and check purchase history.
---

The agent can pay at an online checkout with a card you saved in Sprocket. You approve each purchase with your passkey, unless you turned on auto-approval in Agentcard. [Agentcard](https://agentcard.sh) stores your cards, and Sprocket never sees the card number.

## Save a card

Open **Settings → Payments** and click **Add a card**. Scan the QR code with your phone to save the card on Agentcard's page. Use **Open on this computer** instead if your passkey is on your computer.

## How a purchase works

<Steps>
	<Step title="The agent shops">
		The agent opens the store in its browser and confirms the items, quantity, shipping address,
		shipping method, and total with you.
	</Step>
	<Step title="You approve">
		The thread shows the amount and the store, such as **Approve $24.99 at Adafruit**. Click
		**Approve**, scan the QR code with your phone or open the link on the computer with your
		passkey, and confirm. Purchases that Agentcard auto-approves skip this step.
	</Step>
	<Step title="Agentcard pays">
		Agentcard sends the payment to the store's payment processor. Some payments need you to finish
		on Agentcard's page or complete a bank check. The agent checks the store's result and reports
		the order number or payment error.
	</Step>
</Steps>

The checkout page must use HTTPS and a payment processor that Agentcard recognizes. Approval alone doesn't mean the store accepted the order. If the payment result is unknown, check the store and your card before trying again to avoid a duplicate charge.

Sprocket cancels a pending purchase when the agent's run ends, including when you stop it.

The agent can only start a purchase in a thread you started from the app. Subagents and `sprocket run` tasks can't start purchases.

## Purchase history

**Settings → Payments** lists your last 100 purchases with their status and links to their threads.
